# Slaylist-Suite# 

💋 The Slaylist Suite

> **Books. Vibes. Obsessions. All in one place.**

The **Slaylist Suite** is my personal reading universe — a custom-built home for my books, reading history, TBR, reviews, ratings, favorite reads, buddy reads, reading goals, and all the fictional chaos in between.

Born from my bookshelf inside **The Baddies Bookshelf**, the Slaylist Suite takes my reading life beyond a basic tracker and turns it into an interactive, highly personalized digital library.

Because apparently a 2,000+ book Goodreads TBR needed its own address. 💀📚

---

## 💄 What's Inside the Suite

### 📚 Shelfies & Slaylists
My complete reading library, including:

- 2,000+ book TBR
- Currently reading
- Read
- DNF
- Paused
- Physical books
- Ebooks
- Audiobooks
- Custom shelves and collections
- Search, sorting, and filtering

### 📖 Reading Receipts
The evidence.

Track what I'm currently reading, reading progress, start and finish dates, formats, ratings, and my complete reading history.

### 💗 Certified Slays
Five stars only.

A dedicated home for the books that earned a perfect rating and deserve permanent bragging rights.

### 🧠 Altered My Brain Chemistry
Beyond five stars.

These are the books that moved in, rearranged the furniture, and permanently changed something in my brain.

### 🎀 Reviews & Reactions
Unfiltered thoughts, reviews, commentary, ratings, and the occasional literary crash-out.

### 🏷️ Tags & Tropes
Books can be organized beyond traditional genres using custom tags and tropes — from found family and dark academia to "touch her and die" and whatever hyper-specific category I create next.

### 👯 Booked & Bestie'd
My buddy-reading headquarters.

Keep track of current, upcoming, and completed buddy reads, past reading partners, and books I'm looking for someone to read with.

### 💌 Read With Me
An integrated interest form for readers interested in becoming my next reading bestie.

### 📊 Reading Stats
A visual look at my reading habits, including books read, pages, ratings, genres, formats, tags, goals, and other reading statistics.

### 🎯 Reading Goals
Monthly and annual reading goals with live progress tracking.

### 💋 Monthly Wrap-Ups
Interactive monthly reading recaps featuring books read, pages, ratings, favorites, genres, and other highlights.

Wrap-ups can feature seasonal and themed designs because reading statistics deserve to be cute too.

### ✨ Year in Books
An annual recap of the entire reading year — goals, favorites, statistics, reading eras, and everything that happened along the way.

---

## 🎲 The TBR Situation

With more than **2,000 books** waiting on my Goodreads TBR, the Slaylist Suite is designed to handle a large personal library without turning browsing into a nightmare.

Search it. Filter it. Sort it.

And when choosing becomes impossible:

**Spin the Slaylist. 💋**

A random TBR picker helps decide what gets read next — with optional filters for genre, format, tags, tropes, and more.

---

## 👑 Suite Admin

The public-facing Suite is only half of the project.

A private admin area allows me to manage my library without touching the code every time I read a book.

From the admin dashboard I can:

- Add and edit books
- Update reading progress
- Change reading status
- Rate books
- Write reviews
- Add tags and tropes
- Manage shelves
- Record start/finish dates
- Update reading goals
- Manage buddy reads
- Mark Certified Slays
- Add books to Brain Chemistry
- Manage monthly wrap-ups

---

## 🎨 The Aesthetic

The Slaylist Suite isn't trying to look like a traditional book database.

Think:

**deep plum • black • hot pink • glossy details • Y2K glam • Bratz energy • bookshelves after dark**

The experience is designed to feel like stepping into my personal reading space while remaining functional, responsive, and easy to navigate.

## 💋 Why I Built This

I love Goodreads.

I do **not** love feeling like my reading life belongs inside a spreadsheet from 2012.

I wanted something more personal, more visual, more interactive, and significantly more fun to use — without losing the organization and tracking features I actually need.

So I built my own.

Welcome to **The Slaylist Suite**.

**Books. Vibes. Obsessions. All in one place. 💋**
